## Section 5: Vision & Motivation

### Do you think Europe is a great place to build technology companies?
Europe has the unique "advantage" of high regulation (GDPR, EU AI Act), which most see as a hurdle. I see it as a moat. Building in Europe forces us to solve the hardest problems first—privacy, safety, and governance. If you can build a compliant, ethical agentic system in Berlin, it is ready for the world. Europe’s engineering talent is world-class; we just need to shift from a "consultancy" mindset to a "builder" mindset.

### Why EuroTech?
The EuroTech Federation is the only community that explicitly values "Builders" over "Pedigree." I am an outlier who functions best when surrounded by other technical "Execution Junkies." I am joining to find peers who are building at scale, to leverage the Scaleway infrastructure perks for my agent fleets, and to contribute my red-teaming expertise back to the community via workshops on AI safety.
